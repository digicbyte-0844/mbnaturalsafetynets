# MB NATURAL SAFETY NETS - Image, Media & Visual Asset Art Direction

## 1. Visual Creative Direction & Brand Standards

The visual identity of **MB NATURAL SAFETY NETS** (Proprietor: **Manohar Gosala**, Designed & Developed by: **Prabhuteja Koda**) shifts from amateur hardware photography to **"Modern High-Rise Architectural Safety"**:
- **Aesthetic Tone**: Ultra-clean, sunlit contemporary high-rise apartments in Hyderabad (warm teak decking, glass railings, panoramic skyline views over HITEC City, Gachibowli, and Financial District).
- **Transparency Emphasis**: Photography must clearly highlight the dual benefit: 100% unblocked skyline views and complete fall protection for children, toddlers, and pets, alongside complete pigeon exclusion.
- **Lighting**: Bright natural morning sunlight (10:00 AM sun) or warm architectural golden hour.
- **Human & Emotional Connection**: Happy families, young children, or household pets safely enjoying open balconies without vertigo or anxiety.
- **Hardware Credibility**: Close-up details of marine-grade SS 316 cables, zero-crack nylon wall plugs, and precision turnbuckles.
