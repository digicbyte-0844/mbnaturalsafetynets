## 3. High-Converting FAQ Content

1. **Q: Will the pigeon safety nets block our sunlight or balcony ventilation?**  
   *A*: Not at all. Our pigeon safety nets are crafted from translucent, ultra-thin monofilament copolymer nylon (approx. 0.7mm - 1.0mm thickness). They allow 100% natural light and cross-ventilation while becoming virtually invisible from just 8-10 feet away.
2. **Q: What is the weight capacity and safety of SS 316 Invisible Grills?**  
   *A*: Our invisible grills use marine-grade Stainless Steel 316 wire ropes with a transparent nylon/Teflon coating. Each vertical wire has a tensile breaking strength exceeding 400 kg. They easily withstand impact from children, pets, or high-rise wind loads without sagging or rusting.
3. **Q: How long does the safety net or invisible grill installation take?**  
   *A*: Standard balcony pigeon netting takes about 2 to 3 hours. Complete multi-balcony flats or SS 316 invisible grills typically take 4 to 6 hours. We provide free same-day measurement and next-day installation options across Hyderabad.
4. **Q: What is covered under the 5-Year Warranty?**  
   *A*: Our 5-Year Warranty covers net integrity, UV degradation, anchor bolt tightness, cable tension, and rust-free assurance on all SS 316 fittings. If there is any sagging or wear, our technicians service it free of cost.
5. **Q: Can duct nets be opened for AC servicing or plumbing maintenance?**  
   *A*: Yes. All our duct area installations include heavy-duty industrial zipper access panels and velcro hatches. Maintenance technicians can easily open and close the hatches without damaging or cutting the net.
6. **Q: Are your bird spikes harmful to pigeons or birds?**  
   *A*: No. Our bird spikes are 100% humane and recommended by animal welfare guidelines. The tips are blunt-engineered to prevent perching and roosting without causing physical injury to birds.
